Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. To provide the Service, we store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect bank credentials, card numbers, or any payment information, as Spendly never connects to your financial accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the Service: storing your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and displaying your spending history back to you. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial information for advertising or any other commercial purpose.

3. Data Storage

Expense data is stored on our servers using reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect it against unauthorized access. Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may delete individual expenses at any time, and deleting your account removes your data.

4. Third Party Services

We do not share your data with third parties except where necessary to host and deliver the Service, or where required by law. Any providers we work with are bound to handle your information only on our instructions and with comparable protections to those described in this policy.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the support channel listed on our website. We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries promptly.
